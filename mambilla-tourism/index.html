About Mambilla

The Roof of Nigeria,
Reimagined

Nestled in eastern Taraba State, the Mambilla Plateau is Nigeria's highest and most spectacular plateau — stretching 96 km long and 40 km wide, flanked by dramatic 900-metre escarpments. Year-round cool temperatures, Nigeria's only large-scale tea estates, cascading waterfalls and pristine montane forests have earned it the name "Switzerland of Nigeria."

TheMambillaTourism is transforming this untapped paradise into Africa's premier luxury destination — where natural wonder meets world-class golf, ranching, a crystal lake marina, and private estates.

Crystal Lake Marina

Mambilla Crystal Lake

We are engineering a spectacular 200-hectare artificial lake in the heart of the plateau — creating West Africa's highest-altitude water destination. Fed by natural highland streams, the Mambilla Crystal Lake will maintain pristine, crystal-clear water year-round.

Surrounded by luxury lakefront lodges and dining pavilions, it will transform Mambilla into a complete, all-season destination unlike anything else in Nigeria.
