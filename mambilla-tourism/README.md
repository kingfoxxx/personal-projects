## Overview

**TheMambillaTourism** is a full-featured luxury tourism and real estate website built to showcase and transform the Mambilla Plateau — West Africa's highest plateau at 1,800 metres above sea level — into Africa's premier destination for travel, investment, and lifestyle.

The site presents a bold vision: combining Mambilla's extraordinary natural assets (cool highland climate, rolling green hills, cascading waterfalls, tea estates, and Nigeria's highest peak) with world-class modern infrastructure including a championship golf course, crystal lake marina, highland ranch, casino resort, and luxury real estate.

## About Mambilla Plateau

- **Location:** Taraba State, northeast Nigeria
- **Elevation:** 1,600–1,828 m above sea level
- **Area:** 9,389 km²
- **Highest Peak:** Chappal Waddi — 2,419 m (Nigeria's highest mountain)
- **Climate:** 14–25°C year-round — coolest region in Nigeria
- **Notable:** Nigeria's only large-scale tea cultivation region
- **Rivers:** Source of the Donga and Taraba Rivers
- **Forest:** Ngel Nyaki Montane Forest Reserve
- **Waterfalls:** Gashaka, Serti, Leme, Mbamnga, Bang Central
